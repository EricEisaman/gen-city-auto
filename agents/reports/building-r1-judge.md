# Building (midrise0) judge, round 1: 5.0/10 (round 0: 4.0)
Scores: silhouette 4, relief 5, material 5.5, colour 5, wear 4, distance 5.5, close-up 5, lighting 5, IQ 7.
"From the front it's past PS2 and reads like a PS4 filler building. From the rear, party wall and roof it's still PS2-flat."
Top fixes:
1 Rear/service elevation is a punched-hole grid: steel fire stair stack (stringers, landings, railings), 2-3 downpipes roof-to-grade
  with offsets, bracketed condensers + condensate lines, sill/frame extrusion on every punched window, back door, meter box.
2 No crown/roof silhouette from the street: coping cap with drip edge ~5 cm proud, core penthouse a full storey with door/louvre/ladder,
  tank on framed stand, perimeter railing, 2-3 condensers, optional rooftop sign frame (lattice+panel); keep in LOD1.
3 Night: windows uniform cool white, top floor glows as one slab, empty white-box interiors: per-window colour temp presets,
  blind/curtain drawn height with slats, ~40% off, interior prop silhouettes (desk, shelf, person); warm shopfront w/ counter, menu boards.
4 Street level no storytelling: lit fascia sign band, roll-shutter housings, A-frame sign, door push bar, real vending machine mesh
  (recessed lit display, coin panel, slot), noren, plants, stickers.
5 Weathering only macro blotches: sill-anchored streaks, 0-40 cm splash band, efflorescence on brick, rust bleed under brackets/sign mounts.
6 Party wall has punched windows, ACs, frosted panels lit at night: no openings; party-wall material (block/skim coat, waterproofing
  patches, ghost roofline of demolished neighbour, tie-rod plates, a vent/drainpipe at most).
7 Too few signs; LOD1 shell drops signs/vending (colour pop): projecting sign per tenant floor, window vinyl lettering, bake sign quads
  + emissive into LOD1.
8 Rain is flat darkening: wet sheen on ledge/coping tops, animated rivulet normals on walls and glass (stronger under sills),
  stronger glass Fresnel/reflection, pooled darkening at wall base. Also glass by day is flat dark grey with weak reflection.
Broken: building sunk into grade on rear/party sides (paving cuts through bottom row of rear windows; no plinth, no door) - see rear-day;
LOD1 drops signage; party wall lit openings at night; minor canopy/mullion aliasing.
